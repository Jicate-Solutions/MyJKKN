A dedicated mentor, he has guided 40 M.Pharm and 22 B.Pharm students and served as a Ph.D. co-guide for six scholars.
He has played key roles in institutional development as NAAC and NBA coordinator, and as head of Entrepreneurship and Alumni Cell. An active member of professional bodies like the Indian Pharmaceutical Association, he continues to advance pharmaceutical education, research, and innovation.
