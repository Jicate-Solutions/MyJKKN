1. **Run falsifiable tests in hour 1, not hour 6** — 11 of 15 artifacts could have been prevented by running the Bharathiraja test before building the infrastructure to support its conclusions. (saved as `feedback_falsifiable_test_before_infrastructure.md`)

(saved as `feedback_skill_creator_first_for_new_skills.md`)
3. **Auto-load memory must encode rules/audit gates, NEVER predictive claims** — predictive claims self-reinforce after refutation, requiring full demotion banner cleanup. (saved as `feedback_auto_load_no_predictive_claims.md`)
4. **Anti-feature audit is needed on every skill upgrade** — structure improvements ≠ scope expansion; v2 rigor adds without auditing what was kept out is the failure mode. (already saved earlier this session)
5. **The 4-test rubric (Colgate/Hyatt/Juicero/RXBAR) is the only artifact that survived self-audit** — the other 14 artifacts depended on the predictive thesis being true. The rubric doesn't.
6. **Demotion > Deletion** — 11 demoted artifacts with banners are the receipt that the falsifiable-testing discipline worked. They serve future sessions as a worked example of "what happens when you skip the empirical test."
