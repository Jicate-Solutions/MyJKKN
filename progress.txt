Cheap cue-stack achieves ~80% of the perceptual effect for ~5% of the GPU cost. This is cross-paradigmatic: borrowing from cognitive science (predictive coding) + graphics (compositor pipeline) + product (Indian mobile market hardware reality).

**Hierarchy of treatments — universal application kills the magic.**
80% Tier A flat / 15% Tier B subtle / 4% Tier C glass / 1% Tier D shimmer is the ratio that makes special moments feel special. If everything shimmers, nothing shimmers (signal-to-noise crashes). Cocktail-party / forest-fire / music-dynamics paradigm.
