### Key Decisions Made During Interview

| # | Question | Decision | Rationale |
|---|----------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | Feature priority | All features at once | Module needs to be complete |
| 2 | LC structure | One unified LC across all 9 institutions | Per real JKKN governance model |
| 3 | YUVA structure | Chapter per institution + CRUD verticals | Based on Yi-style chapter model |
| 4 | Terminology | JKKN progressive (Learner/Senior Learner) | LC module leads the shift |
| 5 | Interview feature | LC Selection Process (not performance review) | Resolved contradictions in spec |
| 6 | Selection flow | YUVA via interview; LC via progression; Exec via rotation | Three distinct processes |
| 7 | Data visibility | Filtered by default (institution), toggle to LC-wide | Balance privacy with cross-institution needs |
| 8 | AI Prod House | NOT in scope (Solutions Hub handles it) | Already exists in MyJKKN |
| 9 | Senior Learner Advisor | Same as Faculty Coordinator | Review/recommend, no approval power |
| 10 | Issue Management | Enhance existing Grievance module; LC links to it | Grievance serves all users (not just learners) |
| 11 | Messaging | Full chat for LC/YUVA only Phase 1 (~150 users) | Manageable scope with Supabase Realtime |
| 12 | AI level | Rule-based now, AI-ready architecture | Pragmatic, reliable, upgradeable |
| 13 | OD approvals | Configurable chains per institution | Institutions have different workflows |
| 14 | Term management | Full tracking with history and handovers | Position history important for governance |
| 15 | Venues | Integrate with existing Resource Management module | Venue booking already exists |
| 16 | LC vs YUVA verticals | Different structures | Portfolio Heads = governance; Verticals = activities |
| 17 | Announcements | LC + YUVA chairs only, scope matching authority | Resolved contradictions in spec |
| 18 | URL structure | /learners-council/ with own dashboard + home widgets | Clear module boundary |
