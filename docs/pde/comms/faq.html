PDE — Frequently asked questions

Common questions from learners, faculty, parents, and employers about the Principal Development Engine.

Basics

Is PDE replacing my grades?

No. Your degree and marks continue exactly as before. PDE is a separate transcript that runs alongside your academic transcript.

What does PDE stand for?

Principal Development Engine. It is JKKN's framework for recording learning that happens beyond formal coursework — leadership, judgment, embodied skill, community work, and so on.

How is this different from the old PDE?

The old PDE was built around assessments and badges. The new PDE is built around demonstrations — pieces of evidence you submit, validated by faculty or peers against a published rubric, organised into seven categories.

What are the seven categories?

Judgment, embodied, problem-finding, accountability, social leadership, cultural and civic, and credential. The faculty and learner explainers describe each in detail.

Participation

Do I have to participate?

Participation is encouraged, not mandatory in year one. The college expects most learners to submit at least one demonstration per semester by year two.

What if I am in a hostel and cannot access stable internet?

Hostel kiosks and mobile-first capture are being rolled out as part of the Tier 5 roadmap. Until then, the desktop computers in each college's library are usable. Submissions are small — usually under 5 MB.

Can I submit a demonstration for something I did last year?

Yes, within reason. Older work is accepted if you have evidence — photos, certificates, witness signatures. The validator uses the same rubric regardless of when the work happened.

Are old certificates and badges recognised?

Yes, via the PDE bridge. Old PDE certificates and capabilities are mapped to the new seven-category framework. See the PDE bridge admin or ask your coordinator.

Validation

Who validates my demonstration?

Either a faculty member from your institution or a peer validator, depending on the rubric. Peer-validated categories (such as peer mentoring) explicitly route to a peer reviewer.

How long does validation take?

Target is seven days from submission. If a validator does not respond within seven days, the submission escalates to the head of department.

What if I disagree with the validation?

You can appeal through your faculty mentor. The mentor reviews the rubric application and either upholds, adjusts, or asks for a second validator.

Can faculty reject my demonstration?

Yes, with a reason. Common reasons: insufficient evidence, evidence not authentic, work does not match the chosen category. You can resubmit with more evidence or under a different category.

The transcript

Can I see my PDE transcript?

Yes, at /learn/pde/transcript. It updates as new demonstrations are scored.

What is the agency-index?

A single number that summarises your overall PDE engagement across the seven categories. It is shown on your transcript and is useful in placement conversations as a quick signal.

Can I download my PDE transcript as a PDF?

Yes. The downloadable PDF is NAAC- and NBA-ready and carries the institutional seal. It is signed digitally.

Who can see my PDE transcript?

You, faculty at your institution, and anyone you choose to share the PDF with. Outside parties — employers, universities — see it only when you send it to them.

Recognition

Will employers actually look at this?

JKKN runs an active employer briefing flow as part of placement preparation, so employers know what to expect. Adoption is growing. The transcript also stands on its own — it is a verified document signed by the institution.

Will foreign universities accept it?

Most admission committees accept supplementary institutional documents. The PDE transcript is one such document. It is most useful when paired with a personal statement that references specific demonstrations.

Does PDE count toward NAAC or NBA accreditation?

Yes. The institution uses PDE data as evidence for several criteria under both bodies. Validated demonstrations feed directly into accreditation reports.

For faculty

How much time will validation take per submission?

The first one takes around 10–15 minutes. After that, most validations take 3–5 minutes once you are familiar with the rubric.

Do I have to validate demonstrations from learners I do not teach?

Yes, sometimes. The system routes based on category and rubric, not only on your teaching relationship. The rubric is published so you do not need prior context.

What if I am too busy this week?

Submissions auto-escalate after seven days if not actioned. You can also flag yourself unavailable in your validator settings to pause new routings.

Still have questions?

Write to pde@jkkn.ac.in or speak with your PDE coordinator. Audience-specific explainers: faculty, learner, parent.
