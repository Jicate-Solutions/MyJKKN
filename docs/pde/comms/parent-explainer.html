What is this new "PDE" thing my child mentions?

A simple note from JKKN to the parents of our students.

In one line: alongside your child's marks, the college now also keeps an official record of what they actually do — leadership, community work, character, and skill. This note explains what it is and what it means for your family.

The simple answer

Your child's degree shows their subjects and marks. That has not changed.

Starting now, the college also keeps a second record. This second record notes the broader things your child does as part of growing up at JKKN — leading a project, helping a junior student, working with a community group, learning a new practical skill, taking responsibility for something difficult.

We call this record the Principal Development Engine, or PDE for short. It sits beside the regular degree certificate.

Why this matters for your child's future

Two things have changed in the last few years.

Employers now ask for more than marks. They want to see if a student has led something, solved a real problem, or worked well in a team. Until now, students had no official way to show this. They had to rely on letters, photos, or word of mouth. The PDE record gives them an official document, signed by the college.

Foreign universities ask for the same. If your child considers higher studies abroad, admission committees want evidence of activity beyond the classroom. Indian universities are also moving in this direction under the new education policy.

Without this record, students lose marks they should have got — not in their degree, but in the next stage of life.

What your child will receive

At the end of their course, your child will receive two documents:

  • Their normal degree certificate, with marks. Unchanged.
  • A separate PDE transcript — an official college document listing the broader work they did, verified by a teacher reviewer.

Both documents carry the college seal. The PDE transcript can be shown to employers, scholarship committees, and admission offices.

How you can help

You do not need to do anything technical. Your child handles the submissions online. What helps the most is encouragement:

  • If your child mentions a community project, a competition, a leadership role, or a new practical skill — encourage them to record it on the PDE.
  • Encourage them to participate in college activities, peer mentoring, and local community work. These are the things the new record values.
  • Reassure them that this is not extra homework. It is recognition of what they are already doing.

Things you might be wondering

Is this extra study or extra fees? No. There are no extra classes and no extra fees. The PDE simply records work your child already does as part of college life.

Will my child's marks be affected? No. Marks come from the regular course, exactly as before. The PDE is separate.

What if my child is shy or does not lead activities? The PDE has seven categories. Some are about leadership, but others are about practical skill, character, language, and judgment. Most students naturally fit at least three or four categories. Faculty will guide them.

Is this safe? Who sees my child's information? Only college faculty and your child can see their submissions. The PDE transcript is shared only when your child chooses to share it — with an employer or a university, for example.

Questions?

Talk to your child's class teacher, or write to us at pde@jkkn.ac.in. We respond to parent enquiries within two working days.
