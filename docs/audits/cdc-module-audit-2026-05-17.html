2. What CDC actually does (workflow inventory)

Across ~283 messages reviewed, CDC runs four parallel workflows, each currently glued together by Google Chat + Google Sheets + Google Drive + Google Forms + manual Word/PDF circulars.

Workflow A · Campus Recruitment Drives

  • Companies sighted: Premier Fine Linens, L.G. Balakrishnan & Bros, Sakthi Auto Components, Rinex Edutech (10 LPA), Rinex Technologies, JICATE Solutions, Dharan Hospital, Foxconn, Datazoic, Sanmar Group, MathWorks, KC Overseas, Pavana Abroad Studies, 4ovation.
  • Lifecycle: Company outreach → Campus Circular (Word doc) → Eligibility/Willingness collection (Google Sheet) → Poster + Promo Video (Jicate Designers) → Drive day attendance (manual headcount per dept) → Technical/HR rounds → Selection results → Offer letter → Placement Day Celebration.
  • Cross-institutional: JKKNCET + JKKNCAS often bid together for the same drive (Sakthi Auto wanted 50 from CET + 30 from CAS).
  • Department-wise attendance is tracked verbally on chat: "AIDS – 4/5, BCA – 4/6, Computer Science – 4/5, Commerce – 19/19, TFD – 12/12…". This is the kind of state a database should hold.

Workflow B · Internships & Industry Immersion

  • Sakthi Auto (60-day, Jan–Mar, ₹12,000+ stipend), Foxconn (₹15K stipend), MathWorks Industry Immersion, Dharan Hospital recruitment drive.
  • CDC distributes internship certificates after completion (JKKNCAS – 5 learners, JKKNCET – 2 learners receipt from chat).
  • Required-document checklists run via chat ("Mark Sheet, ID Card, Aadhar, 4 Photos, Bank Passbook in 2 copies each").

Workflow C · Career Guidance Outreach to Government Schools

  • "AI Horizons: Discover, Design, Do — Unlock Opportunities and Career Pathways" — 2-hour workshops at Government Higher Secondary Schools (Veppadai, Chithode, Sankari, Magudanchavadi, Pallipalayam, Kumarapalayam, JKK Rangammal Girls School).
  • Sample size scales: 95, 120, 74, 55, 283 (two-school day), 12th-standard students.
  • This is admission-funnel outreach disguised as career guidance. CEO explicitly said: "Note: This would further enhance in reaching the students and parents looking for ADMISSION". The poster says "Why JKKN?" — it's brand recall for the JKKN-2026-27 admission campaign.

Workflow D · Internal Learner Development

  • IDP (Individual Development Plan) for first-year freshers — 297 students, captured via Google Form, "auto-generates a digital report" for 3-year career-customised plan.
  • Passion mapping: Robotics 18, App-dev, Coding, Craft, Culturals, Sports, ML, AI, Entrepreneurship.
  • Clubs spawned from passion chart: Solar, IoT, Circuit Design, Robotics, IIC Program.
  • Senior-fresher mentorship pairings; "Tech Spotlight" weekly sessions.
  • Aspiration tracking — 5 dept toppers present "their aspiring companies (Wipro, Google, Tesla)" to juniors.
  • Skill stack push: Infosys Springboard, Python/C training, communication, AI tools (NotebookLM, Gemini, Claude — yes, by name).
  • Unnati Training Programme — 90-hour structured training for final-year aided students with valediction event.
  • External opportunity bulletin: ISRO Scientist drive, SBI Youth for India Fellowship, DRDO 6-month internship, MRB pharmacist exam prep.

4. Codebase audit — does MyJKKN already cover any of this?

Full sweep across app/(routes), lib/services, types/, supabase/setup/01_tables.sql, supabase/migrations/, and lib/sidebarMenuLink.ts:

Existing moduleWhy it's not a fit for CDC
/admissionPre-enrolment funnel only. Lead → application → admit. Doesn't model corporate recruiters or learner outcomes after admit. No company table, no drive table.
/learners + /learners/alumniLearner profile + alumni filter. Alumni route is a stub list view, not a placement system. No placement history, no skills/aspirations, no IDP.
/academicTimetables, attendance for CLASS sessions, periods. Does not model "training programme" as an entity. cdc_drive_attendance is a different shape from student_attendance (different lifecycle, different rules).
/hr/recruitmentHires EMPLOYEES (faculty, staff). Reverse-flow from CDC: CDC manages COMPANIES that hire LEARNERS. Table shapes are not compatible. Reusing would break HR semantics.
/staffStaff records; CDC head/coordinator are staff rows but the module has no operational surface for placement.
/organizationsInstitution + dept hierarchy. Has a "placement" contact-type slot on institution-form.tsx (just a name+phone field for placement officer). No recruiter table.
/resource-managementReusable for venue booking ("Senthuraja Hall", "Vibrant Arangam", "ThinkTank Room"). Should be a touchpoint, not a host.
/startup-studioHas startup_events, event_registrations, event_venue_assignments, event_team_attendance. Tempting reuse target — but the startup-studio lifecycle is innovation → demo → judging. CDC lifecycle is recruiter → eligibility → selection → offer. Forcing one schema onto both will produce a mess. Treat startup-studio's patterns as a design reference, not a host.
/application-hubSaaS app catalogue for the MyJKKN platform. Unrelated.
/admission/settings/templates/documentsHas a category enum entry placement: 'Placement' — purely a document template label. No data behind it.
Verdict: build a new CDC module. Zero applicable schema exists today. The chat workflow has 4 distinct sub-workflows (drives, internships, school outreach, IDP/clubs) with their own lifecycles. None fits cleanly into existing modules. Forcing fit into /hr/recruitment or /startup-studio would corrupt those domains' semantics.

6. Decisions Director must lock before any DDL

Per /assumption-thrash discipline — these are not technical questions; they shape the schema permanently.

  1. Recruiter scope: Are companies platform-global (shared across all 8 institutions) or institution-scoped? Chat says shared (JKKNCET+JKKNCAS bid together for Sakthi Auto). Recommend: platform-global with per-institution preferred-partner flag.
  2. Drive type taxonomy: On-campus, off-campus, walk-in, virtual? Plus "Industry Immersion" (MathWorks-style observation visits — non-recruiting) — separate entity or drive_type='immersion'?
  3. Internship vs Placement: Same record with outcome_type enum (internship/PPO/full-time), or two tables? Sakthi Auto chat shows internship → potential placement transition.
  4. IDP ownership: Replace Google Form completely with native MyJKKN page (full owner), or deep-link to existing Google Form + ingest responses via Forms MCP?
  5. Willingness consent: Chat says "fill up by learners & parents going to Sakthi Auto". Should willingness require parent_consent_url field?
  6. Photo gallery: Native upload to Supabase Storage, or keep storing Google Drive folder URLs?
  7. School workshop → admission funnel: Should workshop attendees become admission_leads with source='cdc_school_workshop'?
  8. Placement statistics for NAAC: Auto-export to NAAC Criterion 5 (placement) and AICTE annual return formats? (If yes, fields must match those reports' columns.)
  9. Multi-institution access: Is CDC a central HQ that operates across institutions (one CDC head, multiple coordinators) or each college has its own independent CDC?
  10. Existing CDC head role: Robert Maria Vincent is already in staff records — promote to cdc_head custom role? Check if such a role exists.

8. What we should NOT do

  • Don't extend /hr/recruitment for student placement. Different domain. Mixing breaks both.
  • Don't fork /startup-studio's schema. Reference its patterns, but build a separate table family.
  • Don't migrate every old Drive folder retroactively — start from Phase-2 drives onward; let chat history stay archival.
  • Don't kill the chat space on day-one. Use it as a notification destination for 1–2 sprints until users adopt the UI.
