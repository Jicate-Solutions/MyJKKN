## 4. MyJKKN-oriented solutions

### 4.1 Group A — certificates, transcripts, mark sheets (the only real COE item)

**What already exists.** `/service-requests` gives learners an online application, a multi-step approval workflow, a timeline per request, and (since 2026-09-05) certificate PDF generation for Bonafide and Course Completion.

**What the live data shows** (Bonafide + Transfer Certificate + Course Completion, test types excluded):

| Measure | Bonafide | Transfer Cert. | Course Completion | Total |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Requests | 784 | 149 | 55 | 988 |
| Marked fulfilled | 3 | 0 | 1 | **4** |
| Approved, never marked fulfilled | 444 | 94 | 24 | **562** |
| …of those, older than 7 days | 422 | 90 | 24 | **536** |
| Waiting for approval | 102 | 39 | 14 | **155** |
| …of those, older than 7 days | 82 | 38 | 14 | **134** |
| Median days, submit → approve | 3.0 | 0.1 | 0.0 | |
| 90th percentile, submit → approve | 19.6 | 2.1 | 1.0 | |

The oldest approved-but-open request dates from 2026-05-14.

**What this means.** The students' complaint is confirmed by MyJKKN's own data, but the cause is not "manual processing". Approval is usually quick. The request then stays at *Approved* forever: the office either has not issued the certificate or issued it on paper and never pressed *Mark Fulfilled*. From the learner's screen both look the same — no status update. That is 562 learners looking at a request that never closes.

**Gaps to close:**

1. **Fulfilment is not being recorded.** 4 of 988. Make *Mark Fulfilled* part of handing over the certificate; downloading the certificate PDF could mark it automatically.
2. **No message to the learner.** No requester notification was found in the approval service, the service-request API routes, or the database triggers. Add a push / WhatsApp message on approved, ready-for-collection, and rejected.
3. **No turnaround target.** `service_requests` has no due-date or SLA field. Add target days per service type, an "overdue" filter on `/service-requests/approvals`, and a daily digest to the approver. The 134 requests waiting more than 7 days for approval would surface on day one.
4. **Certificate PDFs are switched on for only 2 service types**, one of them a test type. The 6 live Bonafide types and the Course Completion types still depend on the office typing the letter. Enable the existing templates on them (admin setting on the service type, no code).
5. **No service type exists for the COE documents the idea actually names** — transcript, mark sheet (duplicate / consolidated), provisional or degree certificate. These are the part that belongs to the Controller of Examinations. Add them as service types routed to the `coe` role, with the fee step where a fee applies; mark data comes from the COE application.

Items 1 and 4 are configuration and habit, not development. Items 2, 3 and 5 are small builds on an existing module.

### 4.2 Groups E and F — expiry of medicines and chemicals

**What already exists.** `/ims` Inventory has per-item expiry tracking (`track_expiry`), batch-level expiry dates, a batches screen and a stock report. Stores are already set up: Dental Patient Store, Dental Student Store, Dental Warehouse, JKKN Pharmacy.

**What the live data shows:**

- 59 of 1,006 items have expiry tracking switched on.
- 23 of 249 stock batches carry an expiry date. Dental Student Store: 0 of 56. Dental Warehouse: 0 of 1.
- JKKN Pharmacy store: 22 of 87 batches have a date, and **3 of those are already past expiry** and still in stock.

**Solution.** Turn on expiry tracking for all medicine and chemical items, enter expiry at goods receipt, and add a near-expiry alert (30 / 60 / 90 days) to the store in-charge. The register the students propose is already built; it is empty. The physical disposal of expired chemicals (group F) is a safety action for the Pharmacy principal and does not wait on software.

### 4.3 Group J — procurement

**What already exists.** `/procurement` has purchase requests, approval flows with amount thresholds, RFQs to vendors, quotation comparison, purchase orders and goods receipt (GRN).

**What the live data shows:** 38 purchase requests (37 in the last 90 days: 31 converted, 7 awaiting approval), 31 RFQs, 36 quotations, 15 purchase orders — and **0 goods receipts and 0 vendor ratings**.

**What this means.** The digital request, approval routing, quote comparison and status tracker in card #22 are live and in use. What is missing is the last step: nothing is recorded as received, so "what is still pending" cannot be answered, and with no ratings there is no basis for a preferred-vendor list.

**Solution.** (1) Make GRN entry mandatory to close a purchase order. (2) Start vendor ratings at GRN; derive the preferred list from them. (3) Enforce a minimum of 3 quotations above a set amount in the RFQ step. (4) Use the existing `reorder_level` field for regularly bought items. Reply to the author that most of the proposal is live and ask them to measure approval days from the module rather than from paper.

### 4.4 Groups C and D — dental appointments, reminders, queue

MyJKKN has **no** patient, appointment or queue module; the only dental pieces are the Inventory stores and billing. This is a new build, not a configuration change.

Suggested pilot before any build: one department, four weeks, a shared appointment sheet with slots per chair, and reminders sent through the existing MyJKKN WhatsApp integration. Record no-shows and waiting time before and after. Card #23 already names the right measures (no-show count from the register, one-week waiting-time sample, incomplete multi-visit treatments). Build a module only if the pilot moves those numbers. Treat #15 (token / queue) as part of the same pilot — it is the walk-in side of the same problem.

### 4.5 Group I — barcode at pharmacy dispensing

Inventory items carry an item code and the module has POS devices and sales, but there is no barcode field or scan-to-verify step. This is an enhancement to the Inventory sales screen plus label printing and scanners. Ask for the baseline first — 3 months of dispensing errors and a one-week waiting-time sample, as the card itself proposes — because none of it is attached.

### 4.6 Groups B, G, H — not software

- **B (broad dental):** six different problems in one sentence. Send back and ask for one card per problem with one number each. Parts of it are already covered by D and E.
- **G (lab flooring, fans):** facilities work. `/resource-management` maintenance (97 schedules, 90 logs in use) can carry the job once the lab is named and costed.
- **H ("lab visit not permitted"):** the real finding is that the associates were refused entry to the lab, so they wrote a fix for a room they did not see. That is a Gemba access issue for the Pharmacy owner, not an improvement idea.

- The same text was submitted by several learners — on 2026-10-06 four learners re-filed text that others had filed in August and September. With no duplicate check, each copy counts as a separate idea.
