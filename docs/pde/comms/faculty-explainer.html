PDE — what's changing and why it matters to your day

A short explainer for teaching faculty across the eight JKKN colleges.

The Principal Development Engine (PDE) is not a new curriculum. It is a parallel record of the learning your students already do beyond their grades — and the part of your work that AI cannot replace is what makes it credible.

The shift in plain terms

For the next decade, AI will handle more of the content delivery — drafting notes, generating practice questions, summarising textbooks. That part of teaching gets cheaper every month.

What does not get cheaper is human judgment. A student claims they led a community project. A learner submits a clinical reasoning case. A peer mentors a junior. Someone has to look at the evidence and say, "Yes, this is real, and here is what it is worth." That work is yours.

PDE gives you a structured place to do it.

What changes for you

  • You validate demonstrations. Students submit short evidence packets — a video, a document, a witness signature. You review and attest authenticity using a rubric.
  • You attest, you do not re-teach. The categories cover learning that often happens outside your classroom. Your role is to confirm it happened, not to deliver it.
  • You surface civic, cultural, and embodied learning that previously had no transcript — language proficiency, community organising, clinical skill, team leadership.
  • Validation targets seven days from submission. The system shows your pending queue at /admin/pde/demonstrations.

What does not change

  • Your courses, syllabus, and academic authority — unchanged.
  • Your grading of academic work — unchanged.
  • Your relationship with learners — unchanged. PDE is additive.
  • Your existing assessments and certifications — they continue. PDE sits alongside them.

The seven categories at a glance

Judgment
Reasoning under uncertainty, ethical decision-making, contextual choice.
Embodied
Discipline-specific physical skill — clinical, surgical, lab, studio.
Problem-finding
Spotting what others miss; framing the question, not just answering it.
Accountability
Owning outcomes, including failures. Visible follow-through.
Social leadership
Peer mentoring, team project leadership, committee roles, community organising.
Cultural & civic
Indian language proficiency, local community projects, tradition, civic engagement.
Credential
Formal certifications, licences, and externally validated achievements.

Where to start

A five-minute walkthrough lives at /learn/pde/demonstrations — that is the learner view, which is helpful to see first. Your validator inbox is at /admin/pde/demonstrations.

No training session is required to begin. The first demonstration you validate will take longer than the tenth. Aim for one this week.

Questions?

Reach out to your institution's PDE coordinator, or contact the office at pde@jkkn.ac.in. Detailed FAQ at faq.html.
